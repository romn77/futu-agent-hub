# Question-driven company research

## Scope

Start from a specific company and question, or derive two or three material questions for a general review. Resolve issuer CIK, exchange/share class and cutoff. Without supplied historical rationale, call the starting view a current research hypothesis.

An institutional lead means “these disclosed managers changed positions”; motives and operating implications remain to be tested. No automatic fund-following recommendation.

## Evidence path

Use qualified evidence already in the session. Otherwise locate the latest applicable annual/interim filing and material event reports, retaining exact accessions. Establish growth, profitability, cash conversion, balance sheet and capital allocation using periods actually available.

Follow the decisive issue into notes: revenue quality → recognition and receivables; expansion → CapEx and commitments; leverage → maturity/covenants/liquidity; dilution → share compensation and share-count basis. Read the relevant original text, including a truncated excerpt's necessary continuation.

Four-quarter totals need four nonoverlapping standalone periods. Derive cumulative flows only on matched bases; never apply the same subtraction to EPS or balances. Material conflicts stay explicit.

For each pillar, state facts, interpretation, real contrary facts and a condition that would invalidate the conclusion. If no substantive counterevidence was obtained, say so; a future hypothetical risk is not completed counterevidence.

Peers are chosen for an explicit business/comparability question. Default at most two comparative peers; they do not automatically become recommended candidates. A named narrower scope takes precedence.

## Market context and valuation

SEC actuals/guidance do not replace analyst consensus or market prices. When price attractiveness matters, obtain a dated research quote and suitable estimate/valuation inputs through existing authorized tools. Separate pre-release expectations from later observations. Missing consensus permits business analysis, not invented beats or mispricing.

When a parent analyst workflow applies, retain its two-model budget and valuation policy. Standalone valuation uses a suitable available specialist method, with executed formulae and sourced inputs. No DCF label on a price-target summary.

## Deliverable and reuse

Lead with a supported conclusion and the research cutoff. Explain how the company earns money, decisive evidence, financial periods, contrary findings, any performed valuation/peer work, and observable next questions. Keep source links and limitations beside the claims they constrain.

Hand off only relevant public findings: question, claim, fact/inference/scenario, actual source/accession/section, reporting period, support, contrary evidence and unfinished work. Portfolio implications are composed in analyst using its own fresh account evidence.

No account access or persistence is necessary. A company-data gap remains local; a focused answer must not masquerade as a completed comprehensive review.
