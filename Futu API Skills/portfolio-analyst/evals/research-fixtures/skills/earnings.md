Use a confirmed company date or a clearly labelled estimated calendar entry to
determine the stage. For a preview, identify expectations, key operating drivers,
disputes and conditional outcomes. For a recap, distinguish reported performance
and guidance from the contemporaneous consensus; do not replace an unavailable
pre-release estimate with a consensus collected after release. Reuse financials.
Calendar queries are market-wide, at most seven inclusive days each. Confirm
material timing ambiguity with `filing company-calendar --symbol ...` and results
with `filing earnings-release --symbol ...`. Do not treat estimated dates as
company-confirmed or provide both stages for the same event by default.
