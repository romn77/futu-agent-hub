Keep each fiscal period and each EPS accounting basis distinct. Compare estimates
at the provided observation dates and report their own contributor coverage,
separate from recommendation counts. For prior zero or negative EPS, report
absolute change and sign transition; do not publish a misleading conventional
growth percentage. Use up/down counts only for their stated lookback and period;
zero total revisions does not support a directional breadth ratio. Missing
coverage is unknown, not zero contributors or evidence of a bearish consensus.
