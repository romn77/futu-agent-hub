# Active advice, opportunities and simulation

Load when the user explicitly requests actionable change, asks for an active/decisive advisor view,
supplies a quantifiable target weight, amount, or shares, or has established that preference in the
available session. An ordinary diagnosis without such context does not authorize advice expansion.

## Take a position supported by evidence

Lead with the most consequential 1–3 judgments: where incremental capital looks most attractive,
what deserves less capital or attention, and why now. Fewer supported judgments are better than
invented opportunities. With sufficient evidence, generic caution or "keep watching" alone is not
a completed answer. A strong view may favor reducing, retaining or reallocating risk; aggressiveness
is not synonymous with bullishness, leverage or turnover. Do not force every holding into
`ADD/HOLD/TRIM/EXIT`.

For each prioritized idea connect the object/comparator, business driver, price or expectations
basis, catalyst/time horizon, strongest opposing evidence and invalidation condition to this
portfolio. Explain the advantage and cost relative to retaining the current position/cash. A claim
of mispricing or consensus disagreement requires actual market/valuation evidence; otherwise label
it a current research hypothesis. Confidence in evidence is not an invented success probability.

## Allocation proposals

Give an evidenced add/reduce/retain direction, priority and funding source/use. Gains, losses,
thresholds and technical levels alone cannot justify it. Missing a numerical target or full model
does not block qualitative advice. Missing crucial evidence limits only the affected conclusion.

Reuse known liquidity, drawdown and allocation constraints. Where they are unknown, explain the
relevant condition briefly and continue useful comparisons; ask a focused question only when it
materially changes sizing or a requested simulation. Conditional target ranges may use explicit
assumptions, never invented user risk tolerance. Distinguish a proposal under those assumptions from
a personally validated allocation. Exact shares/feasibility need reliable price, cash and availability.
Cash also provides liquidity and optionality; do not assume it should all be invested.

For a material disputed opportunity, compare base/upside/downside cases when inputs permit. Read
[valuation policy](valuation-policy.md) before precise values and execute the calculation. State
business assumptions, period, valuation basis and what would realize each case. Scenario upside is
not expected return; do not probability-weight without an evidenced probability basis. Sensitivities
of one model count as one valuation under [research budgets](research-policy.md), not multiple
portfolio simulations.

## New candidates and offensive strategies

Research unheld new stocks only within an explicit candidate/opportunity request or an established
session preference covering new ideas. Reuse that preference without repeated permission; a later
narrower request takes precedence. Advisor scope does not imply that the user accepts any drawdown
or wishes to use leverage. Follow the research policy's held/candidate/strategy/model budgets.

Compare each candidate with a relevant held name or keeping cash: incremental driver, valuation/
price conditions, catalyst, overlap and contrary evidence. An event calendar is not an investment
edge; dates/stages and the claimed expectation gap require evidence. Do not present a rumor as a
confirmed catalyst. Keep candidate ideas in Observations, outside held tables and held deep research.

An offensive structure—concentration, event exposure, a leveraged ETF or an option—needs a reason
to prefer it to simpler exposure. Explain payoff/loss drivers, appropriate horizon, liquidity and
management needs. Use applicable existing authorized research/calculation capabilities. Missing
contract, quote or margin evidence permits a mechanism comparison and explicit missing inputs,
not fabricated executable contracts, premiums, margin or max loss. The held-options tool does not
validate a new strategy. Do not force a strategy when none meets the evidence/fit requirements.

## Simulation and execution boundary

Every proposal remains non-executable. Never unlock trading, request credentials, or place an order.
Run simulate only for an explicitly requested quantitative rebalance with a user-supplied or
confirmed target weight, amount, or shares. A suggested range does not automatically authorize it.
Normally produce one scenario; multiple rebalance scenarios require an explicit request.
Use the existing [simulation workflow](optional-workflows/rebalance-simulation.md) and contract.
State turnover, cash impact, resulting concentration, assumptions and unavailable fees/taxes/slippage.

Valuation sensitivities or market-shock illustrations do not call simulate or create trade plans.
Do not relabel several allocation plans as sensitivities to bypass the scenario boundary.
