# Conditional SEC research integration

Load for a concrete SEC evidence gap or explicitly requested institutional-allocation context.
This reference supplements the already-loaded research/evidence policies; it does not replace
the fresh account workflow or cause all external research instructions to load by default.

## Select the smallest relevant path

- A company-only question uses the host's company-research skill without
  account acquisition. For one filing fact inside portfolio review, use the shared evidence/tool
  guidance directly; a complete standalone report is unnecessary.
- Requested institution/style context uses the host's institutional-style skill
  with the user's supplied list. No default universe and no full institution scan in ordinary review.
- Reuse qualified public results already available in this session before fetching new data.

When these optional capabilities are available in this repository, their shared references are
`Futu API Skills/sec-research-core/references/evidence.md` and `tools.md`; read only the relevant
reference at its verified installed location. Otherwise use the host-provided catalog/location.
The standalone analyst remains usable without those sibling packages. These are public research
capabilities, not a new portfolio.py research command.
Missing SDK/MCP access is a local research gap, never a reason to install, bypass denial or reacquire
the account.

## Preserve portfolio meaning

Keep the same snapshot_id/as_of, OpenD NAV, cash and every nonzero holding. Do not run a second
helper, analyze, technical or account adapter to support SEC research. Research prices have their
own timestamps and never update bound weights.

Institution signals retain list version, calendar report periods, cutoff, actual coverage,
independent-sample status, supporting/contrary evidence and unresolved work. Match public companies
and themes to current holdings locally. Associated weights are not pure business exposure, measured
correlation or real-time institutional flows.

Within the existing eight-held-name research budget, signals may help prioritize a decisive
question; they do not override explicit names or silently omit material risks. Candidate names,
strategies and the two-model valuation budget remain under existing intent/policy. Receiving a lead
does not count as executing its company research, valuation or simulation.

## Compose one report

Use the established output-contract headings. Put relevant company evidence in 重点研究 and
institutional observations/review triggers in 观察; include actual gaps and omissions in
数据缺口与来源. Institution allocation alone is not a macro fact. Keep candidates out of the
holdings table and distinguish peers from recommendations.

Strong primary evidence can support a clear view, but no 13F signal directly maps to ADD/TRIM,
automatic sizing, orders or saved lifecycle history. Default output remains chat Markdown.

Account identifiers, weights, quantities, NAV, costs, full holdings lists and private notes never
enter external public requests. The standalone research helpers have no brokerage input or action.
